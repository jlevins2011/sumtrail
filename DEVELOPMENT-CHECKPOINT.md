# Sumtrail 2.0 checkpoint

Implemented the requested priorities: four operation-specific visual workshops, guided corrections with separate reporting, saved trails and recovery, a camp progression view with usable lantern rewards, a searchable interactive journal, shared-family learning receipt preparation, and accessibility/offline/release checks.

Existing curriculum IDs, grade placement, profiles, lesson scores, journal records, camp clears, and local legacy credit records remain compatible. New campfires no longer write to the old cross-game credit namespace. The exact package lockfile was installed cleanly; the earlier temporary dependency symlink is gone.

Scored trails remain first-answer fluency rounds. Workshops are exploratory models; corrections are assisted practice. These deliberate boundaries preserve independent assessment rather than treating a revealed answer as mastery.

Shared accounts, cloud sync, website-owned assignments, and server-authorized Lumen Isles credits require the separate family website. The export and identity interfaces are prepared, and the production integration requirements are documented in docs/FAMILY-PLATFORM.md. They are not advertised as active integrations.
